ALCHEMIST'S NOTES

  • Two of the same rough thing, worked together, become something finer.
  • Wood remembers fire; what's left behind is not ash.
  • Water given to stone forgets how to flow.
  • What is forged in embers may awaken a sleeping shard.

HOW TO PLAY

Move with WASD or arrow keys. Trees, rocks, water and glowing shards block your path — click on one while standing next to it to gather its resource.

Visit a campfire to gather fire. Open the Alchemy Lab to combine two ingredients into something new. Not every combination works — experiment!

Follow the notes in the Lab for hints. Your goal is shown at the top of the screen. Craft the final item to complete your story.
